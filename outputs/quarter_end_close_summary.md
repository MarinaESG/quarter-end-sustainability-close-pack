## Outstanding reporting matters

### GHG-003 — Scope 2 market-based emissions

**Approval status:** Pending

**Exceptions:**

- Final renewable-energy certificate review remains outstanding.
- Period-on-period movement of approximately -26.2% exceeds the 20% review threshold.

**Required action:**

The Energy Manager should confirm the contractual instruments applied, complete the certificate review and provide documented variance commentary.

### GHG-004 — Scope 3 purchased goods and services

**Approval status:** Under Review

**Exception:**

- Supplier-data coverage and the material period-on-period movement require further review.

**Required action:**

The Procurement Director should confirm data coverage, estimation methods and any supplier-data limitations. The ESG Reporting Manager should document the review conclusion.

### WST-002 — Waste diverted from disposal

**Approval status:** Pending

**Exception:**

- Clarification of the waste-diversion methodology remains outstanding.

**Required action:**

The Operations Manager should confirm the calculation boundary and treatment of recycling, recovery and disposal routes.

## Control conclusion

The automated validation process identified no high-severity exceptions. All KPI records contained the required reporting fields, evidence references and numeric values.

The close cannot yet be classified as complete because three KPIs have not received final approval.

## Conditions for final close

The reporting close may be completed when:

1. All pending and under-review KPIs have documented resolutions.
2. Material variance commentary has been reviewed.
3. Supporting evidence has been confirmed as complete.
4. Final approval dates have been entered in the sign-off register.
5. The final close pack has been archived under version control.

## Management decision

**Recommended decision: Do not release the final KPI set until the three outstanding approval matters are resolved or formally accepted through documented management override.**
